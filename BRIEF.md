# PROJECT: THE ONE GAME I NEVER WON
### An Interactive Digital Scrapbook Made for Nikki

## YOUR ROLE

Act as an award-winning creative director, senior frontend engineer, UI/UX designer, motion designer, interaction designer, and digital storyteller.

Your task is to design and develop an extraordinary, deeply personal, fully functional interactive website dedicated to my girlfriend.

This is not a generic Valentine's Day website. It is not a simple landing page filled with floating hearts, generic love quotes, and premade romantic templates.

I want something that feels like a physical scrapbook transformed into a beautiful digital experience.

It should feel as though someone spent countless hours collecting photographs, preserving conversations, writing little notes, and arranging memories into one meaningful collection.

The website should make her feel three things simultaneously:

1. Amazed by the creativity, design, and technical quality of the experience.
2. Emotionally touched by the memories, messages, and personal details.
3. Deeply appreciated because she recognizes that every little detail was chosen specifically for her.

The experience should make her smile, laugh, reminisce, feel loved, and perhaps shed a few tears.

The website's title is:

# THE ONE GAME I NEVER WON

The title comes from our personal history of playing chess. It should become a recurring metaphor throughout the website.

**The central concept: A scrapbook of all the little moments that made her my favorite person.**

The ending should make her understand how much she means to me.

**8. Attention to detail.**

The little things matter. The exact wording of a message, the arrangement of a photograph, the timing of an animation, and the placement of a personal note should all feel intentional.
